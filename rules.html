Server Rules

Indy Social is an 18+ Indianapolis community focused on respectful social connection. These rules help keep the server welcoming, safe, and organized.

  1. Everything in its own channel, everything else in ⭐-general.
  2. Respect every member.
  3. Do not send unwanted private messages or friend requests. If you are receiving unwanted PMs or friend requests from a member of this server, contact a @Mod.
  4. This is NOT a place to find a date or hookup.
  5. No inciting or baiting messages in order to start an argument.
  6. Respect pronouns.
  7. Everything NSFW goes to nsfw (ping a @Mod if you are 18+; nsfw is not a porn channel).
  8. Nothing NSFW in public channels.
  9. Any photos posted may be deleted at the request of anyone in the photo.
  10. No selling of any commercial product or service (see classifieds).
  11. No solicitation or promotions.
  12. No selling or rehoming of any pets or living creatures.
  13. No advertising of other Discord servers, subreddits, or groups.
  14. Any channels in the Serious Discussions category must be treated as such.
  15. We are an 18+ community.
