Indy Social is an Indianapolis-based community helping adults meet new people, discover local events, and find others to share memorable experiences with. Indy Social is an 18+ community.

How Indy Social Works

Our community organizes primarily through Discord, making it easy to jump into conversations, discover upcoming plans, and find people who match your vibe. Anyone can create an event they want to share with other members.

Join the Server

Show Up Together

What Is Discord?

Discord is a free community chat platform where people can connect in text and voice channels. Learn more at discord.com. Indy Social uses Discord as its home base to coordinate meetups, share plans, and help members find others for events and experiences. It is available on both desktop and mobile.

Easy to Join

Create a free account, join the server, and introduce yourself at your own pace. Indy Social is an 18+ community.

Organized Channels

Conversations stay clear by topic so you can quickly find event plans and people with shared interests.

Community First

This server is for social connection, not for businesses to advertise themselves or events they are hosting.
